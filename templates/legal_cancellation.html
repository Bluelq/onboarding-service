Cancellation & Refunds Policy

Last updated: {{ cfg.legal.last_updated }}

1. About this policy.

This policy explains your right to cancel and how refunds work. It sits alongside the Website Design Services Agreement; where that agreement and this policy differ, this policy governs cancellation and refunds.

2. The deposit.

The deposit set out in your Website Design Services Agreement secures your project slot and covers the time we set aside and the initial work we carry out. It is non-refundable once work has started, except where you have a statutory right to a refund as a consumer (see section 4). The balance is due before go-live.

3. Cancelling after work has begun.

If you cancel a project once work is under way, the deposit is not returned and you must also pay for any further work completed and any third-party costs (such as domains, hosting, or plugins) we have committed on your behalf up to the date you cancel.

4. If you are a consumer (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013).

If you are an individual buying outside your trade or profession and we agreed the contract at a distance or away from our business premises, you normally have a 14-day cancellation period ("cooling-off period") starting the day after the contract is made. If you ask us in writing to start work during that period, you can still cancel, but you must pay a reasonable amount for the work done up to the point you tell us you are cancelling. If you ask us to begin and the service is fully performed within the 14 days with your agreement, you acknowledge you lose the right to cancel once it is complete.

5. How to cancel.

To cancel, email us at {{ cfg.business.contact_email }} with your name and project details, or use any cancellation form we provide. A clear statement is enough; you do not need to give a reason.

6. Refunds.

Where a refund is due, we will pay it using the same method you paid with, within 14 days of the date the cancellation takes effect (or, for consumer service cancellations, within 14 days of being told you wish to cancel), less any amount properly charged for work already done.

7. Business clients.

If you are buying as a business, the statutory cooling-off right in section 4 does not apply, and cancellation is governed by the Website Design Services Agreement and section 3 above.
